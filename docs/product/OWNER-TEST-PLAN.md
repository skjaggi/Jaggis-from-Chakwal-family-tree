# Owner test plan: before the pilot families

**For:** Sunil, testing the product himself before inviting the 5 to 10 pilot families (pilot planned for April to May 2027).
**Where:** the test product site on sjaggi.com (for example `app.sjaggi.com` and a test family such as `testfamily.sjaggi.com`). The live Jaggi site is never used for these tests.
**Time needed:** about 3 to 4 hours in total, best done over two or three sittings.

## How to use this plan

- Work through the rounds in order. Each line is one thing to try, then what you should see.
- Tick the box when it works. If anything is confusing, slow or wrong, write a short note next to it (a screenshot helps) and send the notes to Claude. Being confused counts as a problem: the pilot families will not be technical either.
- Do each round **on a computer first, then again on your phone**, unless the line says otherwise.
- You need **three email addresses** to play different people: you as the family admin, a "relative" and a "second relative". A spare Gmail address or two works well. Use made-up people (for example "The Testers of Phoenix"), not real family details.
- **Ready for the pilot** means every line is ticked, or what is left is written down and agreed as fine for the pilot.
